Privacy Policy

Innovare · Sacred Europe Watch Faces for Wear OS · Effective 1 November 2026

Sacred Europe Watch Faces ("the app") is a set of six watch faces for Wear OS published by Innovare. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: it does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It contains no code of its own: it is a Watch Face Format package rendered by the Wear OS system. It has no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no account sign-in.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It reads the time and date from the watch only to display them. Tapping the date opens your watch's calendar app; the watch face itself never reads your calendar.

Purchases

The app is sold through Google Play. Google processes the purchase and any refund; we receive no payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, we will update this page and the date at the top before publishing that version.
