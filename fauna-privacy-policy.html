Privacy Policy

Fauna by Innovare · phone app, watch app and the Bubo, Colibri, Testudo and Chamaeleo watch faces · Last updated 10 November 2026

Fauna is a Wear OS watch face collection. The phone app is where you browse the faces and buy the paid ones (Bubo is free); the watch app installs them on your watch. The short version: Fauna does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to Innovare or to anyone else.

Data the watch faces use

The faces are built with Google's Watch Face Format and contain no executable code. To draw the dial, the Wear OS system supplies data that already lives on your watch, such as the time and date, battery level and charging state, step count and heart rate, weather (when a weather source is set up), the moon phase and the contents of complications you choose. This data is read and drawn on screen in real time. It never leaves your watch.

What the Fauna apps do

  • The phone app asks Google Play which Fauna faces your Google account has bought.
  • It sends the list of faces you own, and a request to install a face, to the Fauna app on your own watch. This uses Google's Wear OS Data Layer, which only connects apps on devices paired to each other.
  • The watch app installs the face you asked for using the Wear OS Watch Face Push system service, and can set it as your watch face if you allow it.

None of this information is sent to Innovare. The apps have no analytics, no advertising, no account sign-in and no servers of our own.

Purchases

Paid faces are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and any refund, and we receive no payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The watch app asks for permission to install watch faces and to change the active watch face. You can review or revoke these in your watch's settings at any time. Access to sensors such as heart rate is granted by you to the Wear OS system, not to Fauna.

Children

Fauna is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, we will update this page and the date at the top before publishing that version.
