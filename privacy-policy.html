Privacy Policy

Innovare · Wear OS watch faces (Qamar, Samaa, Hermes, Zaman) · Last updated 2 November 2026

Innovare publishes watch faces for Wear OS. This policy explains what our watch faces do with your data. The short version: they do not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

Data our watch faces use

Our watch faces are built with Google's Watch Face Format. They contain no executable code of their own; the Wear OS system renders them. To display information on the dial, the system supplies the watch face with data that already lives on your watch, such as:

  • time, date and time zone;
  • battery level and charging state;
  • step count and heart rate, as provided by Wear OS and your watch's health platform;
  • weather conditions, when a weather provider is set up on your watch;
  • moon phase, sunrise and sunset, calculated by the system;
  • content of complications you choose to add (for example your next calendar event or notification count).

This data is read and drawn on screen in real time. It never leaves your watch, is never stored by the watch face, and is never sent to Innovare or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. Our watch faces have no network access, no analytics, no advertising and no account sign-in.

Permissions

Access to sensors such as step count or heart rate is granted by you to the Wear OS system, not to the watch face. You can review or revoke it in your watch's settings at any time.

Purchases

Our watch faces are sold through Google Play. Google processes the purchase and any refund; we receive no payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Our watch faces are not directed at children under 13 and collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of a watch face changes any of the above, we will update this page and the date at the top before publishing that version.
